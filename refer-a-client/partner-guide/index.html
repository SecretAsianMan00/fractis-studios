Get Paid For The Connection.

Get paid 5% – 10% commission for any clients you send us. You make the introduction. We handle everything from first contact to final invoice.

The Referral Army.

The Fractis Referral Partner Programme pays commission on every client introduction that becomes a signed contract. Between 5% and 8% of the full contract value. Add active involvement and the ceiling goes to 10% if you are involved landing the client past a warm lead. With yearly retainer loyalty bonuses that compound.

No industry knowledge required. No ongoing commitment. No minimum referral target. You refer leads of companies that can use our services as you get them via our form. We pitch, scope, close, and deliver. Your earnings are outlined in writing and when the client pays, your commission is transferred within 7 business days.

This is not a standard affiliate programme. There is no auto-approve, no generic tracking link, and no race to bring in volume at low rates. This is a selective program. Not everyone gets in. We are Fractis Studios – based in Johannesburg and working with businesses and referral partners locally and worldwide.
