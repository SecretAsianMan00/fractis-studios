01 / Audience

Who is this for?

You already know the right people.

02 / Process

How it works?

Simple from start to payout.

Apply & get approved

Fill in the short application below. We review every application and respond within 3 business days – not a formality, it's how we keep the programme worth being part of.

Sign referral agreement & get your code

Once approved, you sign the Referral Partner Agreement and receive your unique FS-XXXX code. This timestamps every referral directly to you.

Submit referrals via your code

Use the Partner Referral Form with your code and your client's details. From that moment the referral is timestamped and registered to you.

We handle everything

Fractis takes care of all outreach, pitching, scoping, contracting, delivery, and upselling. We keep you informed at key milestones. You don't need to be involved unless you want to (extra 2% commission).

Contract closes, you earn

Once the client signs and pays, your commission is confirmed to you in writing. Payment transferred via EFT or international wire within 7 business days of client payment clearing.

Retainer Loyalty Commission

For monthly retainer clients, commission applies to the first 3 months. Where the client remains active, you receive an annual loyalty commission at months 12, 24, and 36 — calculated on the retainer value at that point, at your applicable base rate. A monthly retainer that grows in value grows in commission at each anniversary.

Active Involvement Bonus

An additional 2% is available where you are actively involved in closing the deal – joining pitch calls, providing meaningful client context, or materially shortening the sales cycle. This is assessed at our discretion and confirmed in writing. A warm intro and one call does not automatically qualify.
