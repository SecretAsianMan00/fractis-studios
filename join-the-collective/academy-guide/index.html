A creative apprenticeship built on real work, real learning, and real outcomes. Not an internship. Not exposure. A real path. You grow by doing – you earn when the work earns its place.

The Fractis Academy.

The Fractis Academy is Fractis Studios' structured creative apprenticeship programme, based in Johannesburg and open to creatives across South Africa and the world.

There is a difference between knowing how to do something and having done it for a client who paid for it. The Fractis Academy closes that gap – whether you are just starting out, switching disciplines, or wanting to grow into new territory with structured learning and live client projects when ready.
