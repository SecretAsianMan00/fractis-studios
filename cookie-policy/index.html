Cookie Policy

Last updated: November 2025

1. Introduction

This Cookie Policy explains how Fractis Studios ("we", "us", "our"), operating from Pretoria, South Africa, uses cookies on fractisstudios.com ("the Site"). It should be read alongside our Privacy Policy, which provides broader context on how we handle personal information.

By clicking "Accept All" on our cookie banner, you consent to the use of analytics cookies as described in this policy. Essential cookies are always active as they are necessary for the Site to function.

2. What Are Cookies

Cookies are small text files placed on your device when you visit a website. They allow the site to recognise your device on subsequent visits and remember certain information about your preferences or behaviour. Cookies do not contain executable code and cannot access other information stored on your device.

Cookies can be first-party, meaning they are set directly by us, or third-party, meaning they are set by an external service operating on our site. They can also be session cookies, which expire when you close your browser, or persistent cookies, which remain on your device for a set period.

4. Cookies We Do Not Use

For clarity, Fractis Studios does not use:

  • Advertising or targeting cookies
  • Social media tracking cookies
  • Fingerprinting or cross-site tracking technologies

5. Managing Your Cookie Preferences

You have several options for controlling cookies:

Via our banner

When you first visit the Site, you can accept all cookies, reject analytics cookies, or open the preferences panel to make a granular choice. You can change your preference at any time by clearing your browser cookies and revisiting the Site, which will re-trigger the banner.

Via your browser

All major browsers allow you to view, manage, and delete cookies through their settings. The links below provide guidance for common browsers:

  • Chrome: support.google.com/chrome/answer/95647
  • Firefox: support.mozilla.org
  • Safari: support.apple.com/guide/safari/manage-cookies
  • Edge: support.microsoft.com

Please note that disabling cookies through your browser may affect functionality on other websites you visit.

Via Google's opt-out tool

You can opt out of Google Analytics tracking across all websites by installing the Google Analytics Opt-out Browser Add-on, available at tools.google.com/dlpage/gaoptout.

6. Your Rights

As a South African resident, you have rights under POPIA regarding personal information processed through cookies, including the right to access, correct, or request deletion of that information. Visitors from the EU or UK have equivalent rights under GDPR.

To exercise these rights or raise any concern, please contact us as set out in Section 8.

7. Changes to This Policy

We will update this policy if we introduce new cookies or change how existing ones are used. The "last updated" date at the top reflects the most recent revision. We encourage you to check this page periodically, particularly if you have previously given or withheld consent.
