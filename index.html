004 / Process

Ways we work.

Flexible ways to work with us — built around your goals.

One-Offs

Fast creative tasks. Strong impact.

Small projects handled by a professional team for an affordable price when needed across any of our services.

Ideal for quick turnaround with smaller campaigns and projects, fixing internal teams' mess or to test the waters before starting something bigger.

Project Based

Focused scope. Defined outcomes.

Clearly scoped projects with agreed milestones, timelines, and deliverables.

Ideal for launches, brand builds, campaigns, redesigns, or standalone initiatives. Often the first step before moving into a longer-term partnership.

Retainer

Ongoing creative partnership. Predictable momentum.

Ongoing access to senior designers, developers and other departments on a monthly basis.

Best for teams that need consistent creative output, fast turnarounds, and strategic continuity — without the cost or commitment of hiring full-time.

006 / Need More

Proof & credibility.

Built on experience. Focused on what's next. Not new to the work — new as a studio.

Fractis Studios brings together senior creatives, designers, strategists, and technologists with decades of combined experience across branding, digital, media, entertainment, and product-driven environments. Before formalising as a studio, our team has contributed to work spanning startups, established businesses, creative brands, content platforms, and long-form media — delivering results across industries and formats. Here is a snapshot of what we have built:

Astral Studios · 2025–Present

Astral Studios

Full RebrandWebsite OverhaulBrand AssetsMarketing

Established in 1991, Astral Studios is a long-standing production company repositioning for a modern, competitive market. We are leading a full brand and digital overhaul — redefining their identity, refining how their legacy is communicated, and rebuilding their website to better showcase their capability and body of work. Alongside this, we are developing a suite of brand and marketing assets to support ongoing growth and visibility across platforms.

Lilla Hardware · 2026–Present

Lilla Hardware

Full RebrandE-commerce StoreRetail BrandingMarketingPhotography

Lilla Hardware is transitioning from a local hardware supplier into a scalable retail and e-commerce operation. We are rebuilding the brand from the ground up and launching a custom online store designed to support nationwide reach and growth into larger distribution. The rollout includes full retail branding — from signage and in-store systems to staff apparel, alongside product photography and ongoing marketing to establish a consistent, recognisable presence.

Batho Fusion · 2025–Present

Batho Fusion (Formally Bizhub I.T Solutions)

Full RebrandBrand AssetsMarketingVideoFundraising Support

Bizhub IT Solutions came to us for a complete rebrand and strategy for aligning the company with a stronger focus on social impact and digital accessibility. Batho Fusion was the result. We created the new identity, messaging direction, and supporting brand assets to reflect this shift, along with marketing materials and video content to support outreach and fundraising efforts. The result is a clearer, more purposeful brand positioned for growth, partnerships, and community engagement.

Everdrift Coffee Co · 2025

Everdrift Coffee Co (Concept Study)

Brand IdentityProduct DesignStore Direction

We designed a full identity system, product packaging, and retail direction to demonstrate how a cohesive brand can extend across physical and digital environments. Luna & Tide Skincare · 2025

Luna & Tide Skincare (Concept Study)

Brand IdentityProduct DesignMarketing Direction

008 / Origins

About us.

Fractis Studios is a creative growth partner built by a creative and a strategist.

Founder & CCO (Chief Creative Officer), Daleen Breytenbach has many years of experience in creative entrepreneurship and design for businesses, companies, and indie projects; bringing deep creative expertise for many different industries.

Co-founder & CEO (Chief Executive Officer), Adam Ryder Brooker comes from a formal business training and technology background, with experience managing teams, projects, systems, and digital operations within high-stakes sectors.

Together, we built Fractis to bridge the gap between strong creative thinking and real business outcomes.

We're based in Johannesburg, South Africa and partner with clients locally and internationally. We operate as a senior-led studio, supported by a trusted network of experienced designers, motion artists, developers, and specialists. We scale when needed, bringing talent to you — no matter the project.

Our work is rooted in clarity, strategy, and execution that holds up beyond launch day — whether we're building brands, campaigns, digital platforms, or creative worlds.
